How it works

Create, organize, study.

Memorization starts when you make the card and sets in when you review it. OCTO keeps both steps simple.

  1. 1

    Write it the way you’d remember it

    Every card has a front and a back, and both are a blank canvas. Sketch a diagram, write out vocabulary, or scribble a formula with your Apple Pencil — anything you can draw, you can study.

  2. 2

    Keep every subject in its place

    Group sets into folders and give each one a color. When it’s time to cram, tap a color to filter down to just that subject.

  3. 3

    Flip, sort, repeat

    Run through a set one card at a time. Tap to flip, then mark each card Learning or Mastered so your next session focuses on what you still need to work on.

Made for Apple Pencil
