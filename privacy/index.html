Privacy policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This policy describes how OCTO Flashcards (“the app,” “we”) handles information when you use the app and this website. It is a practical description for a small personal project, not legal advice.

Who we are

OCTO Flashcards is an independent study app. This site hosts product information, the privacy policy, and the terms of use.

What we collect

  • Account data. Email address, display name, and authentication secrets needed to sign in (handled by Supabase Auth).
  • Study content you create. Folders, flashcard sets, handwriting or drawings on cards, and learning/mastered progress stored so they can sync across your devices.
  • This website. GitHub Pages may log standard request data (such as IP address and user agent) as part of hosting. We do not run a separate analytics product on this site.

How we use it

We use this information to operate the app: create and secure your account and save your cards. We do not sell your personal information.

Processors

Account and database hosting is provided by Supabase. This marketing site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and flashcard data while your account exists. You can delete your account from Settings in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL.
